## Brand & Style

This design system defines an authoritative yet energizing environment engineered for the ambitious student and modern academic. It balances high-velocity gamification mechanics (streaks, real-time peer leagues, dynamic AI study partners) with the crisp discipline of high-end developer and SaaS tooling. Rather than leaning into childish toy-like metaphors, the visual language draws inspiration from precision IDEs, advanced productivity consoles, and mission-critical telemetry dashboards.

The emotional signature is focused, luminous, and empowering. It evokes intellectual momentum: the satisfaction of deep work punctuated by high-voltage achievement markers. Deep, noise-free midnight surfaces minimize retinal fatigue during late-night study marathons, while calibrated neon currents direct cognitive focus toward interactive solving mechanisms, active streaks, and collaborative breakthroughs.

## Colors

The palette operates on an emissive dark-mode canvas grounded in `#0B0F19` (Obsidian Navy). Visual hierarchy relies on calibrated luminescence rather than sheer contrast:

- **Primary (`#2563EB` Electric Blue):** Serves as the anchor for forward momentum, primary navigation, system-level CTAs, and foundational progress indicators.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED` Deep Violet):** Applied to synthetic intelligence workflows, 24/7 AI Tutor interfaces, generative explanations, and cognitive leap milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` Luminous Cyan):** Used for peer-to-peer live signals, online presence, code/solution verification, and collaborative doubt-resolution badges.
- **Accent - Streak Flame (`#F97316` Flame Orange to `#EF4444` Crimson):** Strictly reserved for momentum mechanics, daily streak chains, and critical time-sensitive challenge expirations.
- **Surface Architecture:**
  - `Surface 0 (Canvas)`: `#0B0F19`
  - `Surface 1 (Panels & Shell)`: `#111827`
  - `Surface 2 (Interactive Cards)`: `#1E293B`
  - `Surface 3 (Overlays & Hovers)`: `#334155`
  - `Border / Hairline Grid`: `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured modernity of Plus Jakarta Sans for structural headings with the neutral clarity of Inter for dense curriculum, syntax, problem sets, and interactive forum threads.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings):** Selected for its crisp geometric terminals and modern tech presence. Tight letter-spacing prevents visual drift in metrics and leaderboard ranks.
- **Inter (Body, UI, & Numeric Values):** Delivers clean readability in high-density conditions (code blocks, mathematical breakdowns, nested replies). Tabular figures are enforced for leaderboards, XP counts, and countdown timers to prevent optical jitter.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes an 8pt architectural rhythm, with 4pt subdivisions for microscopic adjustments inside badges, code blocks, and counter chips. 

- **Layout Structure:** 12-column adaptive fluid-responsive layout bounded to a max-width of `1440px` on desktop, shifting to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column flow on mobile screens (`<768px`).
- **Workspace Partitioning:** High-intensity environments (e.g., live AI problem solving alongside a video lesson) implement a split-viewport structure. Panels hold fixed padding of `space-md` or `space-lg`, with internal scroll fields that protect global navigational orientation.
- **Information Density:** Spacing remains compact within data structures (leaderboards, streak meters) to show comprehensive state without excessive scrolling, while instructional content expands into `space-xl` breathing room to aid focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth avoids skeuomorphic drops and instead relies on layered tonal illumination and semi-transparent structural boundaries:

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#0B0F19`. Unelevated.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#111827` background with a 1px border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Active Units):** `#1E293B` background with a 1px border of `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`, with an ambient electric blue aura `0 0 20px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Command Menus):** Backdrop blur of `16px` over `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.82)` with crisp hairline rim lighting `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 4 (Gamified Streak/XP Overlays):** Emissive outer glow leveraging the accent color (e.g., Flame `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.2)` or Violet `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

Roundedness index 2 establishes controlled geometry. Primary modules leverage a baseline of `0.5rem` (8px), expanding to `1rem` (16px) on primary card structures, and `1.5rem` (24px) on focal hero units or modal shells.

- **Micro Components (Badges, Buttons, Inputs):** `0.5rem` (8px). Maintains structural rigor and professional tech posture.
- **Medium Panels (Cards, Doubt Threads, Code Blocks):** `1rem` (16px). Creates soft, digestible containers for complex learning content.
- **Streak & XP Counter Pills:** Full pill (`9999px`) to create distinction between functional workflow containers and gamified status objects.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px corner radius. High-stress interactive state invokes an inner top edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`) and a focused cyan-blue glow on hover.
  - *AI Assistant Trigger:* Gradient transition from `#2563EB` to `#7C3AED`, subtle pulse on standby.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Transparent base, 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#E2E8F0`. Hover triggers background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` and border brightening.

- **Streak & XP Badges:**
  - Fully radiused pill shapes with dark semi-transparent fill (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.85)`).
  - Streak pill utilizes an active flame gradient border (`#F97316` to `#EF4444`) with vibrant orange text and micro-animated particle effects on sequence completion.
  - League division badges display cut-gem metallic vector accents with tier-dependent perimeter glows (Diamond, Obsidian, Platinum).

- **Cards (Course Modules & Community Doubts):**
  - Background `#111827`, hairline boundary `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`, 16px corner radius.
  - Hover state translates card `-2px` along the Y-axis and activates an electric-blue border transition (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`).
  - Thread state chips indicate "Unsolved" (Cyan stroke), "AI Answered" (Violet tint), or "Community Verified" (Emerald glow).

- **Inputs & 24/7 AI Terminal Console:**
  - Monolithic dark fields (`#0B0F19`) with inset drop-shadows for recessed depth.
  - Focus state highlights border with `#2563EB` along with a 3px outer ring at `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
  - Rich formula and inline code inputs use monospaced metrics with custom syntax highlighting tokens.

- **Leaderboards & League Tiers:**
  - Row-based list system with zebra background tones using pure alpha steps.
  - Top 3 podium rows incorporate metallic edge trims (Gold, Silver, Bronze) with electric backlighting and real-time movement velocity indicators (green chevron up / red chevron down).
